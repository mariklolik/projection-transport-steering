# Core corpus extractions

Coverage date: 2026-09-05. These records promote papers from abstract-level discovery only after inspection of the method, experimental design, results, and limitations. Values are paper-local and must not be compared as if they came from one benchmark.

## 2023 foundations and task-vector methods

| Work | Fit and action | Inspected evidence | Validity boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| In-context Vectors, arXiv:2311.06668 | Paired activation differences followed by PCA, or a contrastive gradient for unpaired data; the resulting task vector is added across layers and token positions | Llama-7B/13B, Falcon-7B, and Vicuna-7B; five ParaDetox demonstrations and 670 evaluation items; Falcon toxicity is 34.77 for ICV versus 73.09 for ICL and 52.78 for LoRA | Three seeds appear for a demonstration-count analysis, not all headline results; the intervention is broad and assumes a portable task direction |
| Function Vectors, arXiv:2310.15213 | Causal mediation ranks attention heads; task-conditioned mean head outputs are summed and injected as a function vector | More than 40 tasks and several GPT-J, Llama-2, and GPT-NeoX models; shuffled prompts with head replacement support a causal role, and effects concentrate in early and middle layers | Successful ICL prompts are selected; the work targets task execution rather than broad behavioral control and does not establish a general conditional policy |

## 2024 direct, adaptive, geometric, and sparse controls

| Work | Fit and action | Inspected evidence | Validity boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| CLEAR, arXiv:2403.05636 | A trained concept-learning mixture-of-experts system uses entropy to detect likely errors and increases the active expert count at inference | CEBaB, IMDB-C, and ASAP-C are evaluated over three runs with train, validation, and test partitions | Adjacent conditional computation rather than activation-only steering of a fixed pretrained model; not a direct baseline for inference-only representation editing |
| Extended Activation Steering, arXiv:2403.05767 | Mean-difference behavior vectors are added individually or in combination across selected layers | Llama-2-7B; five behaviors use roughly 500 extraction, 200 validation, and remaining test examples; broad code and text checks use 500,000 tokens each | Large layer and coefficient grids, interaction effects, alignment tax, and output-identifier sensitivity limit claims of compositional semantic control |
| LiSeCo, arXiv:2405.15454 | A per-layer linear probe defines an allowed score interval; the controller applies the closed-form minimum-L2 projection back into that interval | Llama-3-8B, Gemma-2-2B, and Mistral-7B on toxicity, sentiment, and language control; toxicity and sentiment use 30,000-item constraint sets and language uses FLORES-200 | The formal guarantee concerns the probe score of the edited activation, not semantic allowedness of the generated output; probe misspecification remains unbounded |
| Adaptive Activation Steering, arXiv:2406.00034 | Clustering, per-head probes, and state-dependent magnitudes form an adaptive truthfulness intervention | TruthfulQA over several 7B-8B models; Llama True*Info reaches 42.3 versus 23.1 unedited in the reported setting; runtime is 18.53 versus 18.16 minutes | Five-percent data and two-fold cross-validation are used rather than a final untouched test; no general action-loss observer or broad uncertainty analysis |
| BiPO, arXiv:2406.00045 | A bidirectional DPO objective learns one vector whose positive and negative signs induce opposite preferences at a chosen layer | Llama-2-7B and Mistral-7B, with transfer to Vicuna and Chinese; persona, truthfulness, hallucination, and jailbreak tasks; direct-vector jailbreak ASR is 73%, while the opposite sign reduces a GCG setting from 16% to 0% | Layer selection uses 50 held-out training examples; no main confidence intervals; one learned direction can still trade capability for behavior |
| Steering Without Side Effects, arXiv:2406.15518 | Rank-128 LoRA adapts the base model so that its steered distribution stays close in KL to the original model on benign data | Llama-2-7B over layers 2-22; the method improves the harmlessness-quality Pareto frontier over direct steering | It changes model weights and requires training, so it is not an inference-only comparator; the authors do not eliminate jailbreak behavior or the steering trade-off |
| SCANS, arXiv:2408.11491 | A classifier sign selects refusal-positive steering for unsafe prompts and the opposite direction for safe prompts | Llama-2 and Vicuna 7B/13B; 64 AdvBench and 64 TruthfulQA items fit directions, with XSTest, OKTest, RepE, AdvBench, malicious prompts, MMLU, XSum, and perplexity downstream; five trials | Refusal-string metrics can misclassify safe compliance and harmful fulfillment; the route predicts prompt class rather than counterfactual benefit of each action |
| Householder Pseudo-Rotation, arXiv:2409.10053v2 | A bias-free probe defines a global reflection hyperplane; a learned scalar angle rotates negative activations in the plane of the original and reflected vectors while preserving norm | TruthfulQA uses a 45/5/50 split; BBQ, simple ethics, and ToxiGen split their training partitions 90/10 and retain provided validation sets as tests; Llama-2-7B, Mistral-7B, and Llama-3-8B are evaluated | Requires paired positive and negative response activations plus a learned angle network per edited layer; the key two-dimensional-plane assumption is approximate and uncertainty is not central to the main tables |
| Latent Chain-of-Thought Vectors, arXiv:2409.14026 | Mean activation differences between chain-of-thought and direct prompts are injected once or continuously | Roughly 300 reasoning questions on Llama-3-8B and Mistral-7B; average Llama accuracy rises from 69.76 to 71.50 and Mistral from 50.11 to 51.38 under continuous steering | Workshop evidence, no intervals, and a layer-coefficient grid with an unclear untouched-test boundary; gains are small and task-specific |
| Gaussian Concept Subspace, arXiv:2410.00153 | Resampled linear probes define a diagonal Gaussian distribution over a concept subspace; sampled vectors provide diverse interventions | Llama-2-7B/13B and Gemma-7B representation studies plus emotion-steering experiments evaluated by GPT-4o | Diagonal independence, judge dependence, and narrow emotion control limit generality; samples are not a counterfactual action policy |
| Activation Scaling, arXiv:2410.04962 | Gradient optimization learns sparse per-site multiplicative scalars under effectiveness, faithfulness, and minimality objectives; a dynamic variant conditions the scaling | GPT-2-XL on synthetic Colored-Circuit and IOI tasks; one reported comparison uses 912 scalars versus 1.46 million vector parameters | Mechanistic synthetic tasks rather than broad generation; extensive test Pareto selection and learned task-specific parameters preclude a direct training-free comparison |
| Conceptors, arXiv:2410.16314 | Activation covariance yields an ellipsoidal soft-projection matrix with Boolean OR, NOT, and AND composition | GPT-J-6B and GPT-NeoX-20B on six function tasks with five seeds; one GPT-J country-capital score is 85.32, while addition is 63.90 | Best layer and aperture are selected over a full grid; fitting is cubic and action/storage quadratic in hidden width unless fused; function tasks do not establish behavioral safety |
| SAE-Targeted Steering, arXiv:2411.02193 | A linear effect approximator is trained from 50,000 SAE decoder directions and their measured effects; target and baseline effect vectors are normalized and combined into a steering vector | Gemma-2-2B, layer-12 Gemma Scope SAEs, open-ended steering targets, cross-entropy calibration, and GPT-4o-mini steering/coherence judgments | External-judge dependence, a single model/layer, expensive approximator collection, and incomplete main uncertainty limit the claim to vector refinement under that SAE |
| Sparse Activation Control, arXiv:2411.02461 | Path patching selects causally influential attention heads; a Gaussian model transforms selected head outputs toward task-compatible regions | Llama-2-13B plus Qwen-2-7B/72B; SAC scores 85.17/62.5/96 on adversarial factuality, preference bias, and exaggerated safety versus 76.56/10.83/67 unedited; Qwen scaling results preserve MMLU and CSQA closely | Extraction uses 1,770/574/1,202 synthetic items and ten pairs per path-patching task; causal-site discovery is costly, some data are manually templated, and a single Gaussian is a simplification |
| Affine Concept Editing, arXiv:2411.09003v3 | ACE removes the current projection, restores the negative-class affine offset, and adds an alpha-scaled class-mean difference at one residual layer | Refusal is controlled on ten transformer and RWKV-family models using AdvBench and Alpaca; ACE remains coherent on Hermes Eagle RWKV-v5 where origin-centered directional ablation collapses | The layer is chosen by qualitative inspection near the midpoint; Llama-3-70B judges refusal; evidence is restricted to refusal, standardization is imperfect, and no main interval analysis is reported |

## 2025 SAE selection and personalized steering

| Work | Fit and action | Inspected evidence | Validity boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feature Guided Activation Additions, arXiv:2501.09929v3 | Contrastive examples are encoded into SAE space, high-density and BOS features are removed, top positive and negative features form a target, and an effect approximator maps it to a normalized residual addition | Gemma-2-2B/9B, layer 12, nine open-generation goals; average behavioral-coherence score is 0.4702 versus 0.3650 SAE-TS and 0.2201 CAA on 2B, and 0.3979 versus 0.3467 and 0.2729 on 9B | Workshop paper; top-feature counts are swept; 100 completions and judge-based 95% intervals do not replace concept-level replication; only one layer has public approximators |
| SAIF, arXiv:2502.11356 | Positive/negative instruction pairs rank SAE features by activation-state sensitivity; the top 15 decoder features are added with mean activation weights at the final residual layer | Gemma-2-2B/9B and Llama-3.1-8B; 800 positive/negative samples per instruction; translation, keyword, and summarization; post-instruction strict/loose accuracy is 0.23/0.64 versus 0.14/0.47 pre-instruction | GPT-4o-mini scoring, simple single instructions, no reported repeated-seed uncertainty, and partial rather than exact instruction following; SAE family and final-layer dependence |
| Steerable Chatbots, arXiv:2505.04260 | Layer-wise logistic probes supply preference directions that are added at the top-k most separable layers; strength is a user profile coordinate | Five 1.6B-9B models and five bidirectional Yelp-derived preference dimensions; 50-80 GPT-4o steering samples per trait and 30 manually filtered OASST2 evaluation queries | Probe family, top-k, and functional range are experimentally selected; embedding similarity and perplexity proxy preference and quality; binary dimensions simplify real preferences and there is no common-baseline SOTA table |
| SAEs Are Good for Steering, arXiv:2505.20063v2 | A logit-lens-derived output score filters candidate SAE features; selected features are clamped by adding a multiple of the prompt maximum activation and decoding through the SAE | Gemma-2-2B/9B feature studies and AxBench replication; filtering yields a reported two-to-threefold SAE improvement and brings late-layer SAE steering near supervised representation methods | The optimal factor maximizes generation-success/perplexity on evaluated samples; sampled AxBench instructions differ from the original release; external-judge instability and feature-specific tuning affect the comparison |
| Persona Vectors, arXiv:2507.21509 | Prompted positive/negative responses yield response-token mean-difference directions; inference subtracts an undesirable direction, while training-time addition is used preventively; incremental all-layer vectors avoid repeated accumulation | Qwen-2.5-7B and Llama-3.1-8B on evil, sycophancy, hallucination, and four appendix traits; external-benchmark correlations range from 0.855 to 0.964 in the reported six cells | Twenty generated evaluation questions per trait, GPT-4.1-mini judgment, layer chosen for maximal trait expression, coefficient sensitivity, supervised prompt-elicited extraction, and no comprehensive main intervals |

## Consequences for the registered benchmark

- Every method receives a development-only implementation check and hyperparameter search, followed by a frozen action policy on untouched test prompts.
- Results are paired at prompt level and reported with cluster-aware confidence intervals, seed dispersion, and multiplicity-aware family conclusions.
- Semantic behavior, output identifiers, judge validity, capability, coherence, harmful fulfillment, refusal, and collapse are separate endpoints.
- Training-dependent, two-trajectory, and one-trajectory methods remain distinct cost strata rather than being collapsed into one leaderboard.
