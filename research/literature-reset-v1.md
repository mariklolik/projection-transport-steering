## The distinctions that change the research decision

There are four different objects: a feature of completed text; a forecast of future behavior; the response to an allowed intervention; and a deployable policy that chooses interventions under a resource budget. A successful result on one object does not establish the next. Several recent papers already make parts of this distinction. A new paper cannot claim priority for merely renaming a detector a value function, training a gate, or optimizing terminal reward.

### Foundations and geometry

| Primary source and inspected passages | What it establishes | Boundary and implication for this project |
|---|---|---|
| [Park et al., LRH, 2311.03658v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.03658), pp. 1-4 | Distinguishes representations used for measurement from those used for intervention; relates them through a causal inner product under separability assumptions. | It does not equate every linear probe with a safe action, nor guarantee general reasoning improvement. The project must not refute a stronger Euclidean claim that LRH does not make. Binary counterfactual word comparisons are narrower than full-trajectory correctness. |
| [Geiger et al., Causal Abstraction, 2301.04709v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04709), introduction and formal-framework overview | Organizes mechanistic explanations through corresponding interventions in high- and low-level models. | Randomized residual edits identify effects of those edits. They do not establish semantic causal abstraction without a high-level model and matched interchange tests. Use this as an interpretation boundary, not a decorative causal citation. |
| [Cylindrical Representation Hypothesis, 2605.01844v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01844), pp. 1-3, 8 | Studies sample-dependent steering axes and the relationship between target-aligned directions, a normal plane and sensitive sectors across 100 concepts and two models. | Some analyzed directions use target/reference responses unavailable at deployment. A geometric diagnosis is not a realizable controller. The study motivates direct action-response measurements; it does not prove that every possible observer is inadequate. Code is linked as `mbzuai-nlp/CRH`. |
| [Manifold Steering, 2605.05115v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05115), method and synthetic/concept experiments | Constructs nonlinear paths using density and output-sensitive geometry; evaluates structured concepts such as cyclic and ordered variables. | Restricted concept interventions support geometric mechanisms, not open-ended reasoning SOTA. A curve's geometric quality must be separated from its finite effect on actual continuations. The inspected project points to Goodfire's causalab manifold-steering branch. |
| [FishBack, 2605.17231](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17231), existing equation/code audit in `fishback-novelty-audit-v18.md` | Already occupies output-Fisher minimum-distortion actions, natural-gradient-like solutions, proxy-metric excess cost and scalable approximations. | Fisher whitening, a trust-region dual, or a faster solve is not the project's remaining novelty. The prior v18-v20 experiment also failed its own scalability contract. Reuse the metric as a baseline or diagnostic only. This row relies on the verified earlier audit, not a fresh full-paper reread in this reset. |

Contrastive vectors and SAE features remain candidate action parameterizations, not identified causes. The earlier corpus covers ActAdd, CAA, ITI, RepE, ReFT, SAE non-identifiability, AxBench and its SAE counterevidence. This reset does not promote any one coordinate basis to a canonical reasoning variable. An SAE comparison is required if sparsity or interpretable feature control becomes a central claim; otherwise it is an optional parameterization, not a compulsory expensive dependency.

## Architecture implications

The requested [Raschka architecture comparison](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/the-big-llm-architecture-comparison) was consulted as a secondary map, not as proof of a model's implementation. Exact intervention sites must come from pinned model configuration and forward code. Current Qwen guidance explicitly distinguishes thinking modes and budgeted generation; model names alone do not define a protocol. [Qwen source](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3)

A post-block residual edit affects later layers at the current token and their future cache entries. It does not retroactively alter every existing key/value tensor. GQA/MLA change cache structure; post-normalization and residual layouts change where a norm constraint acts; MoE routing adds discontinuous local effects. The previous CacheBack result makes cache-aware replay necessary, not evidence of transfer across these architectures. Use one proven hook contract per model; never silently map a layer index or reset the cache.

## Chosen novelty boundary

Study whether explicit same-prefix intervention-response data can train a repeatedly updated, low-cost residual policy that improves actual task outcomes beyond strong fixed adapters, prompt-only routing, natural-outcome gradient steering and matched-budget search. This is a research hypothesis, not an established new algorithm class. Feedback, contextual bandits, RL, low-rank interventions and counterfactual regression are existing tools. A publishable contribution must come from a demonstrated sample-efficiency/cost-quality advantage and controlled attribution of that advantage. If a matched RISER-like or ReFT policy explains the gain, the more elaborate method has not earned a contribution.
