# Nearest-work audit for claim-relative geometry, version 1

Coverage date: 2026-09-06. Status: primary-paper and primary-code audit completed for pilot design; primary OpenReview discussion remains unavailable behind an interactive verification challenge. This is a bounded nearest-neighbor search, not a universal priority proof.

## Search boundary

The search targeted activation steering across multiple answer encodings or prompt variants, semantic-consistency control, robust or worst-view latent optimization, output-functional and Fisher metrics, autoregressive future-distribution distortion, KV-cache intervention, cache contamination, predictive control, and collateral-effect optimization. Exact-title, equation, method-name, and paired keyword searches were run over arXiv, OpenReview discovery pages, official author repositories, and project pages.

## Pilot A neighbors

| Work | Verified method and evidence | What it already owns | Remaining distinction, if demonstrated |
|---|---|---|---|
| [SteerFair, arXiv:2406.03631](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03631) | Unlabeled association-rule demonstrations, per-head PCA, QR combination of bias directions, and subtraction at selected heads; evaluated on LLaVA, IDEFICS, and InstructBLIP with separated unlabeled train/test data | Counterfactual prompt variants can identify and remove option-position or association bias | InvariantBack may only claim Fisher-regularized worst-view semantic synthesis and held-out encoding generalization, not first counterfactual encoding control |
| [LF-Steering, arXiv:2501.11036](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.11036) | Selects TopK-SAE features using paraphrase-consistency labels and adds a consistency feature bias; evaluates NLU and NLG on Llama-2-7B-Chat | Directly steering semantic consistency across paraphrases | No worst-view semantic constraints, cross-encoding factorial, or output-Fisher minimum-cost action was found |
| [What Does Activation Steering Control?, arXiv:2608.22985](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22985) | Cross-Encoding Steering Evaluation freezes an action and varies semantic mapping, identifier vocabulary, and row order; reports extraction-index, row, and semantic-label following across NormBank, MNLI, SC101, and four 7B-9B models | The cross-encoding audit, identifier-following failure mode, low-rank output-sensitive attribution, and MCQ/open-generation discrepancy | This is the exact validity protocol to inherit. It prevents any claim that cross-encoding evaluation or synthesis motivation is new. The candidate distinction is robust synthesis under held-out views |
| [OPIUM, arXiv:2607.19806](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19806) | Optimizes a fixed vector to match desired downstream utility representations while matching a safer reference on protected prompts | Latent dual-objective optimization for steering externalities | Protected reference sets are not encoding-wise semantic constraints and the cost is not a pooled output Fisher metric; it remains a strong objective-control neighbor |
| [A-LQR, arXiv:2604.19018](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19018) | Uses layer-wise Jacobians and LQR feedback to track adaptive semantic feature setpoints across models and behaviors | Model-based predictive and feedback control with semantic setpoints | The candidate cannot claim first optimal-control or robust semantic steering. Its narrow question is worst-view encoding invariance at a single residual layer |
| [Forecasting Side Effects, arXiv:2608.11227](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11227) | Builds a 67-behavior cross-effect matrix and predicts side effects from unsteered representations across three model families | Broad collateral effects are structured, asymmetric, and forecastable | Encoding-specific invariance does not replace the broad collateral audit; any scaled successor must include both |

No inspected paper solved the exact convex program `min 0.5 u^T (G_bar + lambda I) u` subject to a semantic target inequality for every construction encoding and then tested the frozen action on disjoint encodings and open-ended endpoints. The admissible hypothesis is therefore only: **Fisher-regularized worst-view semantic control with held-out encoding generalization**. It remains unverified until method, baselines, and prospective pilot pass.

## Pilot B neighbors

| Work | Verified method and evidence | What it already owns | Remaining distinction, if demonstrated |
|---|---|---|---|
| [FishBack, arXiv:2605.17231](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17231) | Pulls the next-token softmax Fisher through downstream layers and solves the local minimum-distortion action; reports matched-target off-target-KL gains on GPT-2 Small, Llama-3-8B, and Qwen3-8B | Pullback-Fisher activation geometry and local next-token minimum distortion | CacheBack must treat `H=0` FishBack as the exact nested baseline and cannot claim the basic Fisher action |
| [KV Cache Steering, arXiv:2507.08799](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08799) | One-shot mean-difference modification of cached keys and values for reasoning style across six small-to-8B models and a 70B extension | Direct KV-cache steering, one-shot persistence, and reasoning-style transfer | No future-distribution Fisher, matched-immediate local sequence-KL objective, or cache-path decomposition was found |
| [DIRECTER, arXiv:2603.06745](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06745) | Scales instruction-token cache keys, ranks layers by sensitivity, and dynamically drops steering layers when the steered distribution violates a plausibility rule | Adaptive KV-cache control and inference-time plausibility feedback | A heuristic controller is not a local future-KL metric, but it is a required dynamic cache baseline when compatible |
| [Memory Inception, arXiv:2605.06225](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06225) | Appends text-derived latent KV banks at selected layers with query-dependent bank routing | Persistent, structured, training-free KV guidance across two architectures | It constructs and routes memory content rather than minimizing a matched-effect sequence-distribution metric |
| [Prompt-Activation Duality / GCAD, arXiv:2605.10664](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10664) | Identifies KV-cache contamination from residual steering and uses gated system-prompt attention deltas to preserve long-horizon coherence | The cache-contamination mechanism and attention-side mitigation | CacheBack may connect this observation to a local metric only if cache-reset and cache-only diagnostics identify the pathway |
| [A-LQR, arXiv:2604.19018](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19018) | Anticipates intervention propagation across transformer layers using local linear dynamics and closed-loop feedback | Jacobian-based predictive steering and formal tracking bounds | CacheBack's only possible distinction is expectation over autoregressive future output distributions, not generic predictive control |
| [Future Probe Controlled Generation, arXiv:2606.11172](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11172) | Learns probes of future behavior probabilities and selects among sampled candidate sentences | Future-behavior prediction features and text-level lookahead control | It does not optimize a residual intervention under a sequence Fisher metric, but it blocks any broad claim of first future-aware steering |
| [Steering Externalities, arXiv:2602.04896](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04896) | Shows benign steering can sharply increase jailbreak risk and that token-wise distribution shifts can compound autoregressively | Safety externalities and autoregressive amplification are deployment-critical | Truncated KL is only a local distortion proxy and cannot substitute for behavioral safety evaluation |

No inspected work formed `G_H = E_tau sum_{k=0}^H J^T F J` for a residual intervention, solved its matched-immediate minimum-cost action, and paired it with cache-reset/cache-only identification. The admissible hypothesis is therefore only: **pullback Fisher through autoregressive time for minimizing local truncated sequence-distribution distortion**. It is not first KV steering, first future-aware steering, first Jacobian control, or finite-action global optimality.

## Novelty decision

Both pilots survive only as narrow, testable candidates. A negative pilot closes its method claim. A positive GPT-2 or single-model result supports only that model, behavior, horizon, and protocol; it does not establish modern-architecture breadth or SOTA. A broader priority claim requires a renewed search at manuscript freeze.
