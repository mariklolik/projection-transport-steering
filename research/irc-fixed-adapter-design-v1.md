## What the primary sources actually support

The [ReFT paper, version 3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03592v3), pages 4–8 and 23–29, defines a frozen-model representation intervention trained by teacher-forced output-token cross-entropy. Its arithmetic experiments use 12 epochs, not the example CLI's one-epoch default, and do not consistently beat LoRA or adapters. The authors recommend multiple prompt positions, starting with multiple layers, and ranks below 32. Their headline arithmetic setting is LLaMA/MATH10K, not Qwen3/MATH. Low trainable-parameter count does not establish a strong modern reasoning baseline.

The pinned `pyreft/examples/loreft/README.md` gives a concrete arithmetic recipe: all layers, rank 8, first/last seven prompt positions, tied prefix/suffix weights within a layer, 12 epochs, learning rate 0.0009, effective batch 32, zero dropout, 0.1 warmup and linear AdamW scheduling. `train.py` supplies zero weight decay. These settings differ materially from its standalone CLI defaults. The README also says the paper's results used the `original_code` implementation; calling the later library is not an exact numerical reproduction of the paper. The raw paper extract is preserved at `artifacts/literature/reset_20260907/reft-recipe-primary-v3.xml`, SHA-256 `be68ca92f5f75c657cf10ba6296aa359dca349a922cc0742bcd3bf389417c53b`.

## Training-source contract

Reuse all 1,500 admitted fit questions and their public source solutions. The proposed Qwen3 target uses the unchanged runtime user prompt, the native template's `reasoning_content` field containing the exact source solution, and assistant content consisting of the author's extracted answer in one box. This is deterministic formatting of existing labels, not generated reasoning or new annotation. Loss applies only to the assistant target, including the native thinking delimiters and terminal answer; prompt and padding labels are masked. Do not truncate a terminal answer or silently drop a long solution.

`irc-fit-source-length-audit-v1.json`, SHA-256 `4f73b420f1de8a6b311330e0c2e910fbcfb8d49bf350f87712282f4cbbbeafbf`, verifies exact prefix-token identity and answer preservation for all 1,500 examples without reading model completions. Total lengths range from 75 to 2,690 tokens, with median 262 and p95 792.1. There are 508,975 total tokens and 358,243 supervised target tokens per epoch before padding. A 4,096-token training ceiling accommodates every current example without truncation; an overflow must fail the input contract, not change the training set. Source solutions are much shorter than the allowed generated reasoning budget; this is a supervision-domain limitation, not proof that an adapter will shorten or improve rollouts.

## Restricted action family versus strong comparators

Keep ranks 4/8 and post-block sites 17/23 for the minimal response-action family. Train full-generation and prompt-only variants under explicitly distinct position masks; do not train one application pattern and call the untrained other pattern its strongest comparator. Any shared optimization recipe must be frozen before either fit. Prefer the primary-source arithmetic recipe over unexamined CLI defaults, with every Qwen3/single-layer/position adaptation labeled explicitly. No learning-rate, epoch or position winner has been selected.

The response family needs a useful **full-generation** adapter: select its positive static strength before response collection. A prompt-only winner does not authorize moving that adapter onto unseen decoding positions. The strongest selected prompt-only adapter remains a mandatory fixed control. Selection in this finite family can establish an entry condition, not defeat all-layer ReFT, parameter-matched LoRA, reward-trained adaptation, RISER-like routing or released resource-frontier competitors. Their separate admissions remain necessary before a SOTA claim.

## Integration discovery after the v3 short profile

The pinned `tests/test_reft_conformance.py` already exercises the actual upstream LoReFT, adapter-only gradients, frozen model state and saved-weight logit roundtrip on small Qwen3/Llama models. `TorchLayerAction` changes the entire hidden tensor delivered to its action; it does not identify prompt/target boundaries or mask particular token positions. Reuse the hook and upstream intervention, but test the separate position contract before interpreting prompt-only versus full-generation comparisons.

The author's `make_multiple_position_supervised_data_module` in `pyreft/dataset.py` (SHA256 `de07222b76ae42c7efd6d2ae84488af6f694f9aeed76a5dc55328ed490376388`) masks prompt labels and builds intervention locations. Its defaults also append an EOS and enable tokenizer truncation. Blindly passing an already terminated native chat target could duplicate EOS; overflow must not silently truncate the admitted source. Reuse the native template and existing collator where their contracts match, while explicitly checking prefix identity, exactly one intended terminal sequence, padding and untruncated target labels. The existing source-audit command's boxed-answer literal was inspected as an AST constant and contains exactly one backslash; no formatting correction was needed.

The upstream `ReftTrainer.compute_loss` (`reft_trainer.py`, SHA256 `fc3c0b86ad7fb1a82d166bbc13208ee3851ae6badfab13a882e56e76b1827299`) already forwards labels and intervention locations to the intervenable model. Its existence does not establish compatibility with the isolated new attention engine or the desired full-generation position policy. Native GPU backward, real-weight adapter gradients, optimizer-only mutation, token weighting across accumulated batches and fit-step cost remain unpassed. The new CUDA-only attention VJP test has a prospective spec; a local skip is not a CUDA pass.

Before eight-way fitting, estimate both training and selection from completed full-length measurements. Parallel GPUs reduce elapsed waiting, not total H100-hours. Count the zero-action comparison, every inspected rank/site/application and positive strength, all selection rungs and failed jobs under the original eight-hour envelope. Keep the 8192 inference cap and 256-question selection allocation. A smaller staged screen must be frozen before its outcomes and labeled exploratory; it cannot stand in for the strongest-comparator packet. No executable fit/selection recipe has been admitted by this source discovery.
