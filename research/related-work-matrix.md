## Novelty pressure

Scalar optimal transport alone is not novel after AcT and MiMiC. Conditional gating alone is not novel after CAST, SADI, and PCHI. Metric-aware constrained action overlaps COAST. Feedback control overlaps A-LQR. Nonlinear transport overlaps FLAS, COBRAS, and manifold steering. Prompt-conditioned and token-varying action generation overlaps AlphaSteer, HyperSteer, Steer Like the LLM, and FLAS. Causal component selection overlaps GCM and CircuitSteer.

A defensible contribution must therefore couple elements that are jointly necessary and independently tested. Input-conditioned routing alone is also insufficient after IDEEA. Versions 13-15 close the same-forward action-value route, version 16 closes as a general cross-model mechanism, and versions 18-20 close solver-only FishBack approximation. The only current candidate boundaries are Fisher-regularized worst-view semantic synthesis under disjoint encodings and a future-distribution pullback Fisher with identified cache mediation. Cross-encoding evaluation, semantic consistency, next-token Fisher control, KV-cache steering, future-aware steering, predictive Jacobian control, and cache-contamination diagnosis are all prior work and cannot be claimed separately.
