## Robustness and construct validity

| Work | Audit | Main evidence | Consequence for this project |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adversarial Robustness, arXiv:2606.07696v1 | TextFooler, TextBugger, and BERT-Attack over four extraction methods, six personas, and five 1.5B-30B models | Post-attack target confidence collapses near or below 0.25; directional robustness drops by as much as 64 points; clean-selected optimal layer shifts by as many as 17 positions | Add grouped paraphrase and semantics-preserving adversarial shift; freeze layer selection before shifted test; report robustness by method and architecture |
| Cross-encoding, arXiv:2608.22985 | Semantic labels remapped to disjoint output identifiers | CAA and ITI often follow identifiers; scenario-end extraction reduces identifier effect by 98.3% in the reported setting | Require three identifier alphabets, order permutation, and semantic open-generation confirmation |
| SteerCheck, arXiv:2608.24335 | KL-matched isotropic, PCA, and sign-randomized null interventions | Large apparent effects survive sign randomization and judges can have weak validity | Match construction and KL, not only vector norm; judge reliability is a promotion gate |
| Inverted Detection and Control, arXiv:2608.02957 | Downstream representation response tests vector orientation | ITI-RRF improves 27 of 30 model-concept-direction cells | Calibrate action sign independently from observer discrimination and report anti-steerable fractions |
| Forecasting Side Effects, arXiv:2608.11227 | Sixty-seven-behavior cross-effect matrix | 33%-50% of behavior pairs change significantly while cosine similarity explains at most 19% | Publish the full collateral matrix and protected-tail regressions; a forecaster cannot replace direct measurement |
