## Preserved route history

Outcome-score transport versions 1 and 2 are closed before fit. Version 1 right-censored all 64 Qwen3-8B thinking rollouts at 1,024 tokens. Version 2 used eight parallel H100s for a predeclared 4,096-token termination gate: six of eight responses still hit the cap, while two ended with gold-matching boxed answers that the frozen custom line parser rejected. This separates a termination failure from a measurement-interface mismatch; see [`research/outcome-score-termination-audit-v2.md`](research/outcome-score-termination-audit-v2.md).

Those versions opened no class-mix, observer, transport, steering, or evaluation stage. Version 3 is also closed: non-thinking cuts GPU time materially and yields five terminal boxed integers, but three rows still hit 2,048 tokens and the frozen `[0,999]` parser rejects two unambiguous wrong integers. The complete audit is in [`research/outcome-score-interface-audit-v3.md`](research/outcome-score-interface-audit-v3.md).

Version 4 is closed at its instrument gate. Qwen2.5 resolves termination with 8/8 complete boxed answers at a median 603.5 tokens and 38.7-second parallel wall, but the frozen custom regex rejects four conventional punctuation variants. The full evidence is in [`research/outcome-score-interface-audit-v4.md`](research/outcome-score-interface-audit-v4.md).

Version 5 passed its evaluator and small class-support gates. It preserves the efficient Qwen2.5 protocol and replaces handwritten answer grammars with pinned `math-verify==0.9.0` under a strict boxed-answer configuration audited against both preserved and adversarial cases. The subsequent observer-v1 source packet failed its own gates, as described above. No prior output is relabeled. The method remains a hypothesis, not a validated contribution.

The pinned Latent Reward Steering release was audited before comparator admission. Its published reasoning gains remain frontier markers, but exact released-code reproduction is blocked by a syntax error, absent model artifacts, no inspected held-out reward-model validation split, and dataset-specific sweeps. The project will use an explicitly labeled clean-room LRS-like comparator under the common protocol; see [`research/lrs-source-audit-v1.md`](research/lrs-source-audit-v1.md).

Teacher-forced proxy validity version 1 is closed at its prospective engineering smoke. Gemma/FLAS produced all 14 requested outputs, but the exact Qwen3-14B judge configuration parsed only 13 rows: two explanation-first ratings for one condition truncated at the frozen 160-token limit. The full 1,344-generation packet was therefore not opened, no correlation was estimated, and the proxy-first screening funnel is retired. The failure analysis is in [`research/proxy-validity-smoke-audit-v1.md`](research/proxy-validity-smoke-audit-v1.md).

The public AxBench rule evaluator was audited as a possible objective replacement and rejected before GPU work: it targets narrow surface formats, contains several instruction-to-code mismatches, and does not validate general Concept, Instruction, or Fluency scores. The source evidence is in [`research/behavior-evaluator-source-audit-v1.md`](research/behavior-evaluator-source-audit-v1.md).

ResidualFlowBack version 1 is closed after a complete 12-concept design result: it improves unchanged FLAS but loses to the matched-cost pooled residual and violates the frozen safety limits at every target quantile. The exact adverse result is documented in [`research/residual-flow-design-audit-v1.md`](research/residual-flow-design-audit-v1.md).

Flow-step support version 1 is also closed. Its fixed late-support candidate improves teacher-forced worst-view efficacy over full FLAS, but worsens both neutral endpoints on every concept and loses the matched early-support control. The complete all-subset and Shapley diagnosis is in [`research/flow-step-support-design-audit-v1.md`](research/flow-step-support-design-audit-v1.md).

SemanticOutcomeBack version 1 completed its valid 12-concept Stage A but lost to pooled sequence-metric steering at every frozen factor, so generation and all later stages are closed. InvariantBack failed its frozen mapping-comprehension sentinel before development. CacheBack failed its prospective common-dose coverage gate after exposed development.
