## Coverage closure

Two alphaXiv searches, historical and recency-ranked, were combined with title, backward-citation, and forward-citation sweeps seeded by the foundational and survey papers. Candidates were admitted only after primary-PDF inspection and were excluded when they were input-only, non-language, interpretation-only, or a generic downstream application. Because indexing and preprints change, “full” means full under this documented search boundary through the coverage date, not metaphysical exhaustiveness.
